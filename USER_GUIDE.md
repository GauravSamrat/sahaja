# User guide

- **First launch** offers an empty workspace or editable Operation Independence starter tasks. Use **Start cycle** after waking and **End cycle** when finished. Correct wake time if needed. Midnight does not close a cycle.
- **Right now** uses your energy and time budget. Open **Mode and filters** to set internet, device, dimension and project focus. Choose **Begin**, **Complete**, **Later**, **Edit** or **Something else**. Use **Open time** for quick captures whose estimates are still unknown.
- **Tasks** provides quick capture, search, status filters and editing. In task details, enter one subtask per line. Prefix finished lines with `[x] `. You can archive, reopen and delete tasks. If a timer is running on the task, archiving, deleting or completing it records the elapsed session first.
- **Focus timer** supports pause, resume, stop and complete. Use **Correct duration** to record a revised total and stop. The timer uses elapsed wall time while running, including time asleep or minimized.
- **Activity** logs meditation, rest and naps and shows recent cycles, sessions and weekly totals. Logs are associated with the cycle open when the recorded activity began.
- **Money** tracks INR income and expenses. Pending income appears separately from received income. Correct entries or mark income received when it arrives. The calendar field is the transaction timestamp.
- **Workspace** manages dimensions, project goals, themes, imports and backups. Review the preview before approving selected AI tasks or choosing merge or replace. To reverse a restore, preview the recovery copy and replace with it.
- Press **N** for task details and **/** to focus task search on the Tasks screen.
